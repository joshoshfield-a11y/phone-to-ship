Phone-to-Ship

One payment. Yours to keep, use on your own devices, personal and commercial. No subscription, no account, no telemetry.

What it does

You write a short brief on your phone. Phone-to-Ship plans the build, builds it, tests it, diagnoses what broke, repairs what it safely can, and packages it — then stops and waits for your word before anything ships.

    How it runs

    $ pts orchestrate brief.md
$ pts runs
$ pts verdict <run-id> ship

    Runs in Termux on Android. Python 3.9+, standard library only, no network calls.
