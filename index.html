How it runs

Brief, build, test, decide

Four steps. The last one is always yours.

1. Brief

Write a short brief on your phone — what to build, for what target. The included templates cover the format.

2. Build & test

Phone-to-Ship plans the build, builds the tree, and runs the checks — including a JS syntax check and the Termux packaging checks.

3. Diagnose & repair

Problems are named plainly — missing file, leaked file, JS syntax error, empty tree — with the exact file. The one automatic repair quarantines leaked files; nothing is deleted or edited.

4. You decide

The run stops at awaiting-approval and waits. Ship, fix, or hold — the verdict is yours, and it records a decision only.

$ pts orchestrate brief.md
$ pts runs
$ pts verdict <run-id> ship

Runs in Termux on Android. Python 3.9+, standard library only, no network calls.
